Echo Crew

How do I play?

Swipe to move Zip. Nap copies every swipe two turns later. Both robots must stand on the pad with their own name on the same turn. WAIT is a move too, and often the clever one. A wall stops one robot and not the other, and a blocked command still goes into Nap's queue. Arrow tiles only let a robot in while it moves the arrow's way. The button turns the pads on.

I'm stuck.

Undo is free and unlimited. Tap HINT for the next best move: one is free every day (three with Echo Crew Plus), after that a hint costs 50 coins or a short video. A level can be skipped for 150 coins; it counts as done with no stars, and you can come back for them.

Where did my purchase go?

Open the Store and tap RESTORE PURCHASES. Coins are delivered to the device that bought them. Echo Crew Plus, robot colours and yard looks are permanent and can be restored on any device signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account. If something is still missing, email us with the store receipt and we will put it right.

The daily puzzle

One yard a day, the same for everyone, changing at midnight in your own time zone. Solving it on consecutive days builds a streak. Your best move count for the day is kept, and if leaderboards are turned on in your Game Center or Google Play Games account you will see how you compare.

Ads

Short ads play between levels from chapter two on, never in the middle of a puzzle. Echo Crew Plus removes them for good. Watching a video for a hint is always your choice.

Deleting my data

Progress and coins live only on your device: deleting the app deletes them. We hold nothing about you to delete. See the privacy policy.
