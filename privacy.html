Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026. Applies to every app published by Little Angel Technologies LLC, including Echo Crew.

Our games do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. What follows is the complete picture.

What stays on your device

Your level progress, stars, daily results, coins, chosen looks and settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase was made, so the app can deliver it, and a transaction identifier used to make sure it is delivered once. Apple's and Google's own privacy policies govern the payment itself.

Leaderboards

If you choose to use Game Center (Apple) or Google Play Games (Google), your scores and your nickname are sent to that service and shown to other players on its leaderboards. Those services are operated by Apple and Google under their own policies. You can turn them off at any time in your device settings, and the game works the same without them.

Advertising

Our free games show advertisements served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, general device information, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. On iOS we ask your permission before any cross-app tracking, and declining does not limit the game. Where the law requires it, you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can choose non-personalised ads. Google describes its use of this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Buying the Plus upgrade removes ads between levels.

Diagnostics

If a game crashes, Apple or Google may share an anonymous crash report with us through their developer tools, depending on the choice you made when setting up your device. These reports contain no personal data and are used only to fix bugs.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent any information.
  • We do not build profiles of players.
  • We do not send marketing email or notifications.
  • We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. Our games are not directed at children, and if you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete the advertising identifier in your device settings at any time.
  • Decline tracking (iOS) or opt out of personalised ads (Android) in your device settings.
  • Sign out of Game Center or Google Play Games to keep scores private.
  • Delete the app to remove every piece of data it holds.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Material changes will be mentioned in the app's store listing.
